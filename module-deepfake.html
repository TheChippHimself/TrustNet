Deepfakes & Manipulated Media

Discover how AI is used to create hyper-realistic synthetic media, and learn the subtle visual clues to detect them.

What is a Deepfake?

A deepfake is synthetic media where a person in an existing image or video is replaced with someone else's likeness using artificial neural networks. The term is a portmanteau of "deep learning" and "fake".

Distinction

Deepfakes use AI. This is different from "cheapfakes" (traditional photo editing or video splicing) and satire (parody intended for humor, not deception).

How Deepfakes Are Made

1. Source Data

Thousands of images/audio clips of the target are collected.

2. AI Training

A neural network (autoencoder/GAN) learns the target's face patterns.

3. Synthetic Output

The AI superimposes the learned face onto a source actor's body.

How to Spot a Deepfake

Real Video Signs
  • Natural, rhythmic blinking.
  • Skin tone is consistent across the face and neck.
  • Shadows change naturally with movement.
  • Teeth are individually defined when speaking.
  • Hair movement flows naturally with the head.
Deepfake Signs
  • Lack of blinking or unnatural eye movement.
  • Blurring or discoloration at the edge of the face.
  • Lighting on the face doesn't match the background.
  • Teeth look like a solid white block.
  • Frizzy hair looks like a blurry aura.
