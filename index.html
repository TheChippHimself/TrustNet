What is TrustNet?

TrustNet is an interactive educational platform designed to build your digital resilience. In an era where misinformation spreads rapidly, we provide the tools and knowledge needed to verify the truth.

Whether you're trying to distinguish a deepfake from a real video, or figure out if that urgent email is phishing, TrustNet offers practical, hands-on learning tailored for everyone.
